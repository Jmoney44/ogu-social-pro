Sample strategy

Sample strategies, start to finish

How I would plan campaigns for three very different businesses: a restaurant, a real estate agency and a fashion label.

Sample client · Restaurant

From a quiet page to daily online orders

InstagramTikTokWhatsApp Business
Food videos 40%Customer stories 30%Offers 20%Behind the scenes 10%
Starting point
Great food and loyal walk-in customers, but posts go up once a month and online enquiries are rare.
Plan
Post 5 times a week at lunch and evening peak times. Film a week of content in one session. Add an order link to every post and reply to comments and DMs within the hour.
5×Posts per week, planned in a shared calendar
1 hrTarget reply time on comments and DMs

Sample client · Real estate agency

A 4-week launch for every new listing

InstagramFacebookMeta lead adsWhatsApp Business
Property tours 40%Buyer tips 25%Client stories 20%Open houses 15%
Starting point
Listings go up as a batch of photos with a price and nothing else. Enquiries arrive slowly, and many get a late reply or none.
Plan
Launch every listing as a 4-week campaign with a clear story. Pair organic posts with a small lead ad aimed at buyers in the area, and send every enquiry straight to WhatsApp so no lead is lost.
4 wksCampaign for each new listing
15 minTarget reply time on enquiries
WeeklyReport on leads and viewings booked

Sample posts for one listing

The 4-week campaign

Week 1Tease

"Coming soon" posts, close-up details and staging behind the scenes to build curiosity.

Week 2Launch

Carousel and video tour go live, boosted to buyers in the area who match the price range.

Week 3Nurture

Buyer tips, neighbourhood highlights, story polls and a past client's success story.

Week 4Convert

Open-house event, lead ad for private viewings and a WhatsApp follow-up for every enquiry.

Sample client · Fashion label

Turning a new collection into a sold-out drop

InstagramTikTokCreator partnershipsInstagram Shopping
Styling videos 35%Close-ups 25%Creator content 25%Drops & offers 15%
Starting point
Beautiful pieces, but the feed is flat product photos on a plain background. Followers like the posts but rarely ask about sizes, prices or how to buy.
Plan
Treat every collection as a drop. Build a waitlist before launch, release it with styling videos, have three micro-creators wear the pieces, and tag every product so followers can shop straight from the post.
3Micro-creators styling each collection
DailyStories during launch week
WeeklyReport on saves, DMs and sales

Sample posts for one collection

The 4-week campaign

Week 1Tease

Fabric close-ups, sneak peeks and countdown stories. Followers DM to join the waitlist.

Week 2Drop

Launch Reel and shoppable carousel go live. The waitlist gets a link an hour before everyone else.

Week 3Creators

Three micro-creators style the pieces their own way. Their posts are reshared and boosted.

Week 4Sell-through

"Last sizes" stories, customer photos and reviews, and restock alerts for sold-out pieces.
